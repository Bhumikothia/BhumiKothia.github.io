Bhumi Kabariya — who publishes as **Bhumi Kothia** — is a Ph.D. researcher in Environmental Science at the Institute of Science and Technology for Advanced Studies and Research (ISTAR), The CVM University, in Gujarat, India. She is now based in Richmond, Virginia.

Her doctoral research, *"Improving wastewater settling characteristic using bacterial bioflocculant,"* looks for bacteria that secrete natural flocculating substances and asks how those substances can help solids settle out of industrial wastewater. Chemical coagulants do this job today, but they add residual chemicals and sludge. Bioflocculants are biodegradable and produced by microbes, which makes them a promising, more sustainable option. Her work covers isolating and screening bacteria from waste streams, identifying them with 16S rRNA gene sequencing, optimising the nutrients and conditions that maximise bioflocculant production, and characterising the extracellular polymeric substances (EPS) they produce.

That work has so far produced a peer-reviewed article in *Biosciences Biotechnology Research Asia* (2026), five openly licensed datasets on Mendeley Data, four 16S rRNA gene sequences deposited in NCBI GenBank, and the book *Bioflocculating Bacteria: Mechanisms, Applications and Future Perspectives* (2025). She has presented at five national and international conferences, including oral presentations at ICAEBIO-24 and ICMBT-2024. In 2026 she received the Women Researcher Award from Scientific Laurels.

Before starting her Ph.D., Bhumi worked as an Environment, Health and Safety (EHS) Executive at Shree Ganesh Remedies Limited, a chemical manufacturer in Ankleshwar, where she handled environmental compliance, waste management, safety inspections and audit support. Earlier, she trained as a laboratory technician in a multispeciality hospital. She holds an M.Sc. in Environmental Science & Technology, a B.Sc. in Microbiology and a Medical Laboratory Technician qualification.

She brings together hands-on microbiology, analytical laboratory work and industrial compliance experience, and is interested in roles where that combination helps make water treatment cleaner and safer.
